Artist biography

Rooted in real life.

Michael Henderson is a country singer-songwriter from Andalusia, Alabama, writing and performing songs shaped by faith, family, hometown roots, hard work, and the people who make a place feel like home. His sound brings modern country energy together with honest storytelling at the heart of the genre.

With original songs including “Dirtroad Kind of Love,” “Empty Passenger Seat,” “Gas Station Roses,” and “23 Tag,” Henderson’s music reflects the moments, memories, and miles that shape a life.
